# Sprint 9: Legal, Store Submission, Phased Launch

## Goal

Both apps can launch legally and operationally in one locality with rollback plans.

## Acceptance Checklist

- [ ] Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, and Refund/Cancellation Policy are published and linked in-app.
- [ ] GST-compliant invoices are generated for orders, appointments, and in-store bills.
- [ ] Support/dispute workflow lets staff resolve issues without raw SQL.
- [ ] Empty states handle no providers, no orders, no appointments, and unavailable services.
- [ ] Production Razorpay, APNs, FCM, Supabase, Kafka, and Redis secrets are configured.
- [ ] App Store and Play Store listings, screenshots, data safety, and privacy disclosures are ready.
- [ ] Soft launch is limited to one city/locality.
- [ ] Rollback plan names trigger conditions, owner, and service rollback order.

## Verification

- Submit release candidates to internal testing.
- Complete launch readiness checklist and rollback drill.
