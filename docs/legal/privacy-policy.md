# MyPet Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 3, 2026

MyPet collects only the data needed to operate pet commerce, appointments, delivery, billing, support, and launch-readiness workflows.

## Data Collected

- Account data: email, phone, name, role, auth identifiers, and profile metadata.
- Pet and care data: pet profiles, appointment history, visit notes, prescriptions, and uploaded documents.
- Commerce data: orders, appointments, in-store bills, invoices, payments, refunds, payouts, promotions, and reviews.
- Location data: delivery addresses, provider locations, and captain location while online.
- Support data: disputes, support cases, audit actions, and staff resolution notes.
- Device data: push tokens and notification delivery status for reminders.

## Data Use

- To authenticate users and enforce role-based access.
- To process bookings, orders, payments, invoices, payouts, refunds, reviews, and support cases.
- To power discovery, dispatch, reminders, billing, and operational reporting.
- To detect abuse, prevent fraud, and support legal or tax obligations.

## Sharing

- Payment data is shared with approved payment processors such as Razorpay.
- Push notification data is shared with Expo/FCM/APNs as required for reminder delivery.
- Provider, captain, and customer data is shared only where required to complete a transaction, appointment, delivery, payout, or support action.

## Retention And Deletion

Operational and tax records are retained as required for legal, GST, dispute, and audit purposes. Users may request account assistance through support.

## Security

Private service keys must never be shipped in mobile apps. Production secrets are managed outside the repository through deployment secret stores.
