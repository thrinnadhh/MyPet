# MyPet Refund And Cancellation Policy

Effective date: July 3, 2026

## Orders

- Orders may be cancelled before merchant acceptance or before preparation begins, subject to stock and payment state.
- Failed payments remain failed and should not create a successful fulfillment path.
- Delivered orders enter support/dispute review for refund requests.

## Appointments

- Slot holds expire automatically if not confirmed in time.
- Confirmed appointments may be cancelled according to provider policy and operational cutoff.
- Completed visits are not automatically refundable; they require support review.

## In-Store Bills

- In-store billing disputes are handled by merchant staff or Super Admin support cases.
- Stock and bill finalization use idempotency keys to avoid duplicate checkout effects.

## Refund Handling

- Refund mode defaults to manual review.
- Automated refund mode may be enabled only by authorized admin configuration.
- Refund escalations are tracked through support cases and disputes.

## Evidence

Support may request order IDs, invoice numbers, appointment IDs, payment references, delivery evidence, prescription documents, or photos before resolution.
