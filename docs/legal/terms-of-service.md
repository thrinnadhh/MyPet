# MyPet Terms of Service

Effective date: July 3, 2026

MyPet connects pet parents, merchants, veterinary providers, grooming providers, captains, and support staff for pet commerce, appointments, delivery, billing, and support workflows.

## Service Use

- Users must provide accurate account, contact, address, pet, provider, and billing information.
- Providers are responsible for licenses, service quality, pricing, tax details, appointment availability, and fulfillment readiness.
- Captains are responsible for safe pickup, transport, handover, and accurate delivery status updates.
- MyPet may restrict accounts, providers, listings, payouts, or orders when fraud, safety, legal, or operational risk is detected.

## Payments, Payouts, And Taxes

- Customer payments may be processed through Razorpay or another approved payment gateway.
- GST invoice behavior is shown before launch and generated by the relevant service for orders, appointments, and in-store bills.
- Provider and captain payouts are calculated from completed eligible transactions and remain subject to dispute, fraud, and reconciliation review.

## Cancellations, Refunds, And Disputes

- Refund and cancellation handling follows the published Refund/Cancellation Policy.
- Support staff may request information, escalate refunds, review payout claims, and resolve disputes through the Super Admin support workflow.

## Safety And Availability

- Veterinary advice, prescriptions, and medical records are provided by the provider, not by MyPet as a replacement for emergency care.
- The platform may be unavailable during maintenance, incidents, or phased launch rollback.

## Changes

MyPet may update these terms before public launch. Continued use after a published update means the user accepts the updated terms.
