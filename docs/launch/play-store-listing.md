# MyPet Play Store Listing Checklist

## Customer App

- App name: MyPet
- Short description: Shop pet essentials and book trusted pet care.
- Full description: Explain pet store ordering, vet appointments, grooming bookings, reminders, GST invoices, reviews, and support.
- Screenshots: Shop, Vet, Groom, History, Profile/legal.

## Merchant/Captain App

- App name: MyPet Partner
- Short description: Manage pet services, orders, billing, delivery, and payouts.
- Full description: Explain onboarding, inventory, appointment calendar, POS billing, captain delivery loop, earnings, and admin support.
- Screenshots: Home, Bookings, Inventory, POS, Earnings, Admin support.

## Data Safety

- Complete the Play data safety form using `docs/launch/data-safety-privacy-disclosures.md`.
- Confirm location, financial, health-related pet documents, user-generated reviews, and support data handling.
